How to Probe
  1. Insert 3–4" deep at the edge of the root ball — not dead center, not touching the stem.
  2. Blueberries are the one exception to rule 1: don't probe at the root-ball edge — the amended pocket is uniform soil throughout, so anywhere inside it works. Probe mid-pocket; just stay clear of the outer edge where it meets native sand.
  3. Small 2" plugs/seedlings (e.g. the Liatris seedlings): probe shallower — 1–2", not 3–4" — the whole root ball is only about 2" deep.
  4. Reading sits right on the 3/4 border? Pull the probe, move it an inch or two over (still at the root ball edge), and recheck. Still borderline? Default to GREEN/hold — safer to wait than to risk overwatering, especially for blueberries and coontie.
